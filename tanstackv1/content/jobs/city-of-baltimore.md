Recruited as the Mayor's Office of Performance and Innovation's first full-stack engineer, owning technical strategy across every application built for the office.

- Ran requirements gathering with program staff, coordinated across city departments, and sourced data from outside partners to scope each build.
- Designed and built the Opioid Restitution Fund's reporting platform from scratch: a centralized, database-backed system powering quarterly financial, demographic, and performance reporting for the ORF office and its 11 partner organizations.
- Built a citywide 311 service-request visualization tool (React, FastAPI, MapLibre) mapping requests with geospatial clustering; presented at Baltimore City's GIS Day.
- Architected role-based access control (JWT + Django auth) across four user groups, securing sensitive data by role.
- Delivered WCAG/Section 508-accessible UIs across both applications.
- Built quarterly submission locking with admin override to match the program's reporting cadence.
- Migrated legacy Excel reporting into structured Django schema migrations; managed deployments via SSH to Azure-hosted Linux VMs.
- Authored onboarding and training documentation, enabling program staff to adopt the platform independently.
